# Design decisions and departures from the proposal

Each entry records where the build differs from the Final Build Proposal (5 Oct 2026), and why.
Every one is a config value or a single rule, so it can be reverted.

| # | Proposal | Build | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Service pressure at robust z >= 3.5 is High and can put a facility in Resource Action on its own (§7.3, Appendix B) | Pressure alone is capped at **Watch** unless another credible domain is at Watch or worse | §7.3 says pressure is a proxy and must never read as a stockout prediction. An uncorroborated "ACT NOW" on a demand spike contradicts that. | `service_pressure.requires_corroboration` |
| D2 | MoS below the 2-month minimum is High (§7.4) | Below the **emergency order point (1 month)** is High; below the 2-month minimum is Watch | NHLMIS counts stock at review time, when it is at its lowest. In a healthy min-max system stock sits near the minimum then, so "below minimum = High" flagged about half of all facilities. The EOP value must be confirmed against [10] in Phase 0. | `commodity.eop_months` |
| D3 | Any stockout days in the period is Critical (§7.4) | Stockout days **with stock still below the minimum** is Critical; a stockout since resupplied above the minimum is Watch | A facility that ran out but now holds 3+ months does not need emergency resupply. | `engines/risk.py` commodity rule |
| D4 | Penta1 to Penta3 dropout "rising sharply" (§7.5) | Dropout on a 3-month rolling sum vs the previous 12 months, +15 points, Penta1 >= 20 | Monthly PHC counts are too small to compare directly; single-month dropout is mostly noise. | `vaccine.dropout_rise_points` |
| D5 | Penta3 > Penta1 coherence (§7.2) | Checked on trailing 12-month totals | Monthly Penta3 legitimately exceeds Penta1 because of the dosing lag; WHO DQR applies this check to annual totals. | `COH_PENTA3_GT_PENTA1` |
| D6 | Outlier check (§7.2) | Two levels: statistical outlier (z >= 3.5, minor) and implausible value (>= 4x expected, severe -> Low confidence) | A 5-10x spike is almost always a keying error. It should send the facility to verification before anyone acts on it. | `dqr.extreme_value_ratio` |
| D7 | Analysis date not specified | Analysis for month M runs **30 days after M ends**; reports submitted later are invisible for that month | Point-in-time correctness: the engines see only what a manager could have seen on that date. | `calendar.analysis_lag_days` |
| D8 | Not specified | GRID3 facilities with coordinates outside Bauchi (28) are kept but not mapped | They still report to DHIS2; dropping them would remove them from monitoring. | `coord_status` (superseded by D11) |

## 12-state extension and National Command Centre (10 Oct 2026)

| # | Request / proposal | Build | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D9 | 12 states, 2 per zone: Kano, Kaduna, Bauchi, Adamawa, FCT, Niger, **Lagos**, Oyo, Enugu, **Anambra**, **Rivers**, **Cross River** | **Lagos → Ogun**, **Anambra → Abia**, **Rivers → Delta**, **Cross River → Bayelsa** | Step-1 check: GRID3 Health Facilities v3.0 covers 24 states; Lagos, Anambra, Rivers and Cross River are not in it (the LGA boundary layer covers all 37). Same-zone substitutes from v3.0 keep one consistent facility source. Ogun borders Lagos; Abia is the only other South East state in v3.0; Delta and Bayelsa are the only South South states in v3.0. **Alternative not taken:** GRID3 v2.0 (`GRID3_NGA_health_facilities_v2_0`) does cover the four states (public primary: Lagos 572, Anambra 818, Rivers 421, Cross River 1,176) but has an older, different schema (no `functional`, no GRID3 flag counts) and would mix two releases. Switching is a config change plus a v2 field mapping. | `states` in settings.yaml |
| D10 | One state | The state list, zones, GRID3 names and per-state parameters live in `settings.yaml`; every pipeline step takes a state code and reads/writes `data/states/{code}/` | No state-specific code anywhere. Adding a state is a settings entry plus `fetch-grid3`. | `config.py` (`states()`, `StatePaths`) |
| D11 | Single "outside state" flag | Two tags: **Near boundary** (outside its own LGA polygon, within `geography.near_boundary_km` = 5 km; mapped) and **Location unverified** (further; kept in every queue, not mapped). Distance is to the facility's own LGA polygon, the stricter of "state or LGA". `in_state` is kept as a separate column. | A point a few hundred metres outside a simplified boundary is a precision issue, not an error; one 20 km away cannot be trusted on a map but the facility still reports. | `grid3.coord_status`, `geography` |
| D12 | LGA alias table in code | Per-state `lga_aliases` in settings (Bauchi `damban→dambam`, Kano `garummallam→garunmalam`) | Lists live in config; aliases can differ by state. | `states[].lga_aliases` |
| D13 | Synthetic LMIS facility codes `BA-LMIS-xxxxxx` | `{GRID3 state code}-LMIS-` + 6 hex characters, lengthened for a whole state only on a collision | Bayelsa had a 6-character hash collision. Bauchi codes are unchanged. | `grid3._lmis_codes` |
| D14 | One seed | Seed = `simulator.seed + seed_offset` per state (Bauchi offset 0), and per-state parameters (`report_range`, `timely_range`, `lmis_range`, `supply_range`, `base_opd_median`, `malaria_burden`, `season`) drawn in the original order | Bauchi with default parameters reproduces the original simulation exactly (regression test); other states are independent and individually reproducible. | `simulator.state_defaults`, `states[].sim` |
| D15 | Status label per state (requested: "status label in text") | Four labels mirroring Risk x Confidence at state level: **Act and verify**, **High action load** (ACT NOW share >= 17%), **Evidence gap** (Data Verification share >= 36%), **Watch** (neither). Thresholds set near the middle of the simulated state range. | A first setting (10% / 33%) put 9 of 12 states in one label and told a reader nothing. "Watch" rather than "Stable": a state below both thresholds still has PHCs to act on. Always shown as icon + text. | `national.status` |
| D16 | National view | State aggregates only, precomputed at build time (`mart_state_month`, `mart_zone_month`, `mart_national_month`); counts are summed and every rate is recomputed from summed counts | National totals then equal the sum of states by construction (tested), and a small state cannot pull a national rate toward its own. `/national/states` is ~5 KB gzipped. | `sentinel/national.py` |
| D17 | Map colour | Single-hue sequential blue, 5 equal-interval classes over the 12 states; darker (light theme) / brighter (dark theme) always means "needs more attention", so reporting completeness runs high → low. States not in the demo are neutral grey and labelled "Not in demo" (legend and tooltip). | One reading rule for every metric; no red/green; status colours stay reserved for queue state. | `NationalMap.tsx` |
| D18 | Routes | `/` role switcher, `/national`, `/national/briefing`, `/state/{code}`, `/state/{code}/lga/{id}`, `/state/{code}/facility/{id}`. Old `/lga/{id}` and `/facility/{id}` redirect; old single-state API paths remain as deprecated aliases for the default state. | Every view is one click from the landing page and shareable; nothing that linked to the Bauchi build breaks. Brief, metric and filters are kept in the URL (`/national?state=kano&metric=pct_stale`). | `web/app`, `api/main.py` |
| D19 | Access | No login in the demo. A pilot adds real authentication with state-scoped access, enforced in the API as well as the interface (About page). | Judges must reach any view logged out. | About page |
| D20 | Database | `shm_size: 512mb` on the PostGIS container and `ANALYZE` instead of `VACUUM ANALYZE` after load | At 12 states (3.4 GB) the default 64 MB `/dev/shm` is too small for maintenance work. | `docker-compose.yml`, `db.py` |
| D21 | (Action layer) When cases open | The simulated clock starts on **2026-10-30**, the day the September 2026 assessment is produced (analysis runs 30 days after month end). Cases open from the latest analysis month only. | A case cannot open before the data that triggers it exists. Older months are history, not a backlog. | `simulation.clock_start` |
| D22 | Live status | A separate overlay, `mart_facility_live` = baseline + fresh field verifications. `mart_facility_month` and `mart_queue` are never written. With no verification, live equals baseline for every facility (tested on all 12 states). | Keeps the assessed record auditable and the Bauchi regression exact, while the operational view moves with the field. | `actions/live.py`, `service.refresh_live` |
| D23 | Confidence after a field count | A fresh count (≤ 14 days) lifts confidence to **Moderate at most**, and only when every severe quality reason is a stale NHLMIS report. A missing report, silence or incoherent values keep Low confidence until the real report is ingested. A field-confirmed shortage is tagged ACT NOW (field-verified) even under Low confidence; a working fridge lowers the vaccine domain. | A self-reported count is evidence, not an audited report: the four safety guarantees continue to hold in the live layer. The verification *is* the "verify" in Verify-then-act. | `verification.*` |
| D24 | R10 state response | **Suggest**, not open: eligible states are flagged on the national view and a national persona opens the case ("Request state response"). The national level never auto-messages a state. **NEEDS OWNER REVIEW** | A federal request to a state is a political act; a person should decide to make it. | `rules.R10_state_response.mode` |
| D25 | One rule per group | Rules are grouped (resource, verification, vaccine, session, LGA); within a group the first matching rule wins, groups are independent. Dedupe on (unit, playbook) with a 14-day cooldown unless priority rises. | A facility gets one commodity case and one data case, not five overlapping ones. | `rules[].group`, `dedupe` |
| D26 | Priority modifiers | Modifier rules (R11 access, R12 hidden burden) raise priority by one step, at most once each, and never open a case. | Access and burden change *how urgent*, not *whether* to act. | `rules[].mode: modifier` |
| D27 | Escalation trigger | Not acknowledged within the SLA of the current ladder step escalates one step and notifies the next person. Once acknowledged, a case escalates only when it is past due **and** idle for the SLA. One step per tick; stops at the top. | Escalating acknowledged, in-date work punishes the people who responded. | `engine.tick` step 8 |
| D28 | Approval | Every outbound message is a draft until a person approves it; simulated people never approve. Resupply requests are pre-filled but always need approval; nothing moves stock automatically. | Ground rule. Tested: no message is sent without `approved_at`. | `messaging.require_approval`, `simulation.approve_probability_per_day: 0` |
| D29 | Provider | One implementation, `SimulatedProvider`. Twilio / Africa's Talking / SMTP classes exist only as stubs that raise `NotImplementedError`; any configured provider other than `simulated` fails at start-up and in tests. | "No real messages, ever." | `actions/providers.py` |
| D30 | Notification volume | P1 notices go immediately; other notices are bundled into one daily digest per person; at most 8 messages per person per day, the rest wait. | Thousands of open cases would otherwise bury an LGA officer's phone. | `messaging.digest`, `max_per_person_per_day` |
| D31 | Persona and scope | No login. A persona cookie chooses who you act as; the API enforces scope (403): national acts on state cases, state on its state, LGA on its LGA's facility and LGA cases, facility staff reply but never approve or assign. | Shows the accountability model without building authentication (D19). | `actions/scope.py` |
| D32 | Reply parsing | Keyword-value parser with one-edit fuzzy keywords, aliases (ACTS, RTD, frigde), number-first order, zero words (nil/none) and fridge words; anything partial or unparseable goes to a human review queue rather than being guessed. | Real SMS replies are messy; a wrong guess would move a live status. | `actions/replies.py` |
| D33 | Demo scenario | `actions reset` always re-seeds four scripted cases per demo state (Bauchi, Kano) in one LGA: shortage confirmed, adequate stock confirmed, fridge repaired, and an unacknowledged case that escalates. All other replies come from the simulator. | The stage loop plays out the same way after every reset, in under 90 seconds. | `actions/demo.py` |
| D34 | Messages are labelled | Every template variant (SMS, WhatsApp, email subject or body) contains "DEMO"; SMS bodies are held to 160 characters by shortening the facility name only. | "Everything synthetic is labelled synthetic" applies to messages too. Tested for every template and every facility. | `message_templates.yaml`, `templates.render` |

## Access and travel burden, Workstream A (10 Oct 2026)

Real-data derived (WorldPop, MAP friction surfaces, GRID3); nothing simulated. Sources and checksums: `docs/data_sources.md`.
Code: `sentinel/access/`. Settings: `access` in `settings.yaml`.

| # | Question | Build | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D40 | Which facilities count as service points | Public primary facilities from `dim_facility`, **minus those GRID3 marks Not-Functional** (105 of 12,924). "Unknown" status counts as open. Alternatives: `functional_only`, `all` | `functional_status` is "Unknown" for ~86% of facilities; "Functional only" would drop most of the network and overstate burden everywhere | `access.facility_filter` |
| D41 | Population year | WorldPop Global2 R2025A **2026** constrained 100 m (a projection). Nigeria total 240.0 M | Most recent product that downloads, and it matches the analysis period. The 2020 constrained product is six years older. Ogun comes out at 11.0 M, well above official projections, because WorldPop allocates Lagos-fringe growth there; it is WorldPop's figure, unchanged. **NEEDS OWNER REVIEW** (whether to prefer a census-anchored year) | `access.sources.population` |
| D42 | Working grid | Per-state Lambert azimuthal equal-area grid centred on the state, **1 km** cells. WorldPop pixels are binned by pixel centre (population conserved exactly; tested). State = union of its GRID3 LGA polygons. Ward and LGA membership is decided at 100 m, before aggregation | Equal-area cells and near-true distances without per-row latitude corrections; 100 m membership keeps ward totals exact while travel time runs at 1 km (12 states in about 20 s) | `access.working_resolution_m`, `compute.run` |
| D43 | Travel-time method | Walking travel time (primary) and motorised (secondary): `skimage.graph.MCP_Geometric`, 8-connected, cost = friction (min/m) x step length (m), seeded at every included facility cell. Friction resampled from ~0.93 km to 1 km by **nearest** | Sensitivity of % beyond 60 min walking to the resampling rule: FCT 0.7% (min) / 1.0% (median) / **2.2% (nearest)** / 2.5% (average); Kano 0.8 / 1.2 / **1.7** / 1.9; Bayelsa 3.5 / 4.4 / **4.8** / 5.5. Nearest keeps MAP's own pixel values without blending slow (1 km/h) pixels into neighbours or erasing them. **NEEDS OWNER REVIEW** | `access.friction_resampling` |
| D44 | State edges | The grid extends **30 km** beyond the state. Routes may leave the state, and facilities of **other covered states** within that ring are valid nearest facilities (278k people across the 12 states are nearest to one). Facilities in states outside the demo and across international borders are **not** available, so burden near those borders may be overstated. **NEEDS OWNER REVIEW** (fetching GRID3 v3.0 facilities for all neighbouring states would close most of the gap) | People cross state lines for care; a hard state wall would overstate burden on every border | `access.buffer_km`, `access.cross_border_facilities` |
| D45 | Travel time inside a facility's own cell | Cost-distance gives 0 minutes there; the build uses the mean within-cell trip, 0.3826 x cell side x friction (about 4.6 min walking at 1 km) | Without it, more than half of Abia's population read as 0 minutes. Facility-to-facility alternatives use the uncorrected centre-to-centre cost | `compute.INTRA_CELL_FACTOR` |
| D46 | Facilities with `coord_status = location_unverified` (231 included facilities) | Keep a row in `mart_access_facility` with `access_computed = false` and null metrics; they do not seed travel time | Their coordinates are more than 5 km outside their own LGA (D11); seeding them would place service where it may not exist | `compute.run` |
| D47 | Threshold boundary | A value **exactly at** the threshold counts as within; only t > threshold is "beyond". Cells that cannot be reached count as beyond every threshold | One rule everywhere; tested | `compute.beyond` |
| D48 | Catchment and alternative PHC | Catchment = population of cells whose nearest facility (by walking time) is that facility; facilities sharing a cell split it equally and are each other's alternative at 0 min. Nearest alternative = minimum over the facility's region boundary of cost(a) + step + cost(b), which is the exact grid shortest path to the next-nearest facility. `isolated` = alternative beyond the main threshold (60 min walking), or no alternative reachable | A catchment is geographic proximity, not observed utilisation. The boundary method avoids one cost-distance run per facility | `compute.boundary_alternatives` |
| D49 | Ward boundaries | GRID3 `NGA_Ward_Boundaries` (3,095 wards across the 12 states). 40 wards that GRID3 marks Invalid with no geometry are dropped and logged. Population outside every ward polygon (258k, 0.3%) is in LGA and state totals but no ward | Ward `lgacode` matches `dim_lga.lga_id` in every state, so no crosswalk is needed | `fetch.fetch_wards` |
| D50 | Candidate locations | Per state, the 10 cells **beyond the main threshold** with the highest walking minutes x population, at least 10 km apart (the threshold filter was added at integration: without it, dense towns 12 minutes from a PHC ranked in the top 10), described as "candidate area for outreach or a new service point" | A ranked list of places to look at, not a siting recommendation: ignores staffing, land, security and cost | `access.candidates_per_state`, `access.candidate_min_separation_km` |
| D51 | National and zone access figures | `mart_access_zone` and `mart_access_national` sum state counts (population, people beyond each threshold, population x minutes) and recompute rates and means. Medians are not rolled up. Every table states that it covers the 12 demo states only | Same rule as D16; national totals equal the sum of states by construction | `compute.build_national` |
| D52 | Parallel `access` command | `access` passes the importable `sentinel.access.compute.run` to the process pool | Under `python -m sentinel` on Windows/Python 3.13, the spawn pool cannot unpickle functions defined in `__main__` (`_state_step`), so `--workers > 1` fails for the existing per-state commands too. Not changed here (shared code). **NEEDS OWNER REVIEW** | `sentinel/__main__.py` |


## Hidden burden, Workstream B (11 Oct 2026)

Modelled from data the system already holds; validated against the simulator's truth (scoring only). Code: `sentinel/burden/`.
Settings: `burden` in `settings.yaml`. Every figure is shown with its interval or its validated lift.

| # | Question | Build | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D60 | What "hidden" means | Expected confirmed malaria cases and OPD visits at PHCs whose monthly report is **missing**; PHCs with fewer than 6 reported months in the window get no estimate and are counted separately | A late or partial report is already in the data; a missing one is the gap a manager cannot see | `burden.indicators`, `min_history_months` |
| D61 | Point estimate | The PHC's own seasonal baseline (the service-pressure baseline: rolling median of prior reported months × state calendar-month index), **scaled by this month's actual / expected ratio among PHCs that did report** | Without the scaling, state totals missed the month's common shock and the interval held in 1 of 12 months; with it, facility error is 14–16% and the state total within 1–3% | `compute.error_quantiles` |
| D62 | Intervals | 80% central interval from the quantiles of (value / expected) / nowcast among reporting PHCs over the trailing 12 months. State, zone and national totals combine facility intervals as independent errors | Learned from the data itself, no truth; validated at 78–80% facility coverage in every state. Totals are narrower than facilities and held 42–92% of months (12 months per state): read them as approximate | `burden.interval` |
| D63 | Stock behind stale NHLMIS reports | Implied stock = last physical stock + mean NHLMIS net flow (received − consumed, in months of AMC) × periods elapsed; sd grows with √periods. Label from P(stock ≤ 0): **elevated_risk** ≥ 0.5, **watch** ≥ 0.3, else no_signal. A first version that ignored deliveries flagged 95% of stale PHCs and was right 9% of the time; replaced. Validated: PHCs flagged elevated ran out 1.4–3.1× as often as the base rate (Bauchi 12.5% vs 4.9%), interval coverage 79–85% across states. **NEEDS OWNER REVIEW**: a ranking signal, not a prediction | Deliveries since the last report are unknown; the label is worded as risk, never as fact | `burden.label_thresholds`, `compute.implied_stock` |
| D64 | R12 | Raises priority one step for a Data Verification case whose ACT label is elevated_risk (131 cases at reset), never opens a case | The signal is weak alone but cheap to act on: a field count settles it | `rules.R12_burden_exhausted` |
| D65 | Truth isolation | Truth tables are read only by `validate`, after the marts are written (tested) | The estimates must be reproducible in a real system that has no truth | `compute.run`, `tests/test_burden.py` |
| D66 | National figure `hidden_malaria_cases` | Sum of state estimates; interval from summed variances | Same roll-up rule as D16 | `compute.build_national` |
